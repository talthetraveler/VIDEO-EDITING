## The 2021 departure and Cairo
- SAFE: In March 2021, 13 Jews from three families left Houthi-held Sana'a and went to Cairo (S3, S4; original report Asharq al-Awsat).
- SAFE: They refused an offer to go to Israel; they first wanted the UAE (S4).
- SAFE: The departure was reported as a deal in exchange for Marhabi's release; he was not released. His mother was among those who left (S3, S7, S10).
- SAFE (attributed only): "An Arab country with no ties to Israel sent envoys to Sana'a and arranged it with Egypt; the Jews live in a closed compound in Cairo and work as silversmiths" - this is S1 alone, the country is unnamed (Israeli censor), no named sources. Say "according to The Times of Israel".
- After March 2021: 4 elderly Jews remained (S3) or 6 including Marhabi (S4). By 2024 "reportedly only one"; Ynet cited local sources saying five (S12).

### Discrepancy (a) - how many left in 2021
- "13 people, three families, March 2021" is the well-sourced number. SAFE to state.
- "Nearly 100 in 2021" appears only in S1 (and blogs copying it). It conflicts with S3/S4, which count 13 leaving and 4-6 remaining. UNSURE. Use only as "nearly 100, The Times of Israel later reported", or avoid.
- Safe script line: "In 2021 the last Jewish families left Yemen for Cairo."

## Levi Salem Musa Marhabi
- Names: Levi / Libi Salem Musa Marhabi (S5, S6). Born c. 1987, Kitaf wa Al Boqe'e district (S10 only - UNSURE; say "in his late thirties").
- SAFE: March 2016, 17 Yemeni Jews were flown to Israel in an operation involving the Jewish Agency and the US State Department, carrying an old deerskin Torah scroll; photos of it in Israel circulated (S2).
- SAFE: Days later a Jewish man in Yemen was detained, with a Muslim airport employee, accused of helping smuggle the scroll (S2). USCIRF: "In March 2016, Houthi forces arrested Marhabi for allegedly smuggling an ancient manuscript out of Yemen" (S5).
- SAFE: Sentenced March 2018 to 3 years 6 months (S5 says 13 March; S10 says 18 March - say "March 2018").
- SAFE: September 2019 a Houthi appeals court ordered his release; only his non-Jewish co-defendants were freed (S5, S6).
- SAFE (reported): kidney and lung problems, lost all his teeth, reports of torture (S5, S7); "partially paralyzed" (S9, quoting State Dept 2022 report citing NGO Insaf).
- SAFE (reported, USCIRF): after 7 Oct 2023 he was moved to a more dangerous ward, went on a ten-day hunger strike, then put in solitary confinement; May 2024 reported physically ill; reports of withheld food, beatings, electric shocks; no family contact (S5).
- Scroll age: 500 years (S2, S3, S4) vs 800 years (S1, S7). UNSURE - say "centuries-old".

### Discrepancy (b) - was he ever in Israel / in the Netanyahu photo
- S1 says he "previously lived in Israel but returned", and was photographed with Netanyahu in March 2016. Wikipedia repeats this citing only S1.
- Against: S2 (written the same week) calls him "a local Jewish man" detained in Yemen; USCIRF, the State Dept and ASF never mention Israel; ASF's director described him as part of a group with no desire to leave Yemen (S7); S15 (snippet only) says S1 is wrong and "he was living in Yemen, not Israel".
- Verdict: UNSURE and probably wrong. Do NOT say he lived in Israel or appears in the 2016 photo. SAFE line: "He was arrested in Yemen, accused of helping them take the scroll out."

### Discrepancy (c) - latest confirmed status
- Latest dated factual report: USCIRF, "In September 2024, it was reported that Marhabi is in solitary confinement and is in poor health." Status field "Not Released". The page was unchanged in the 6 Dec 2025 archive snapshot (S5).
- Later mentions are advocacy with no new facts: Jewish Link (23 Oct 2025 or Jul 2026) says he "remains behind bars"; Aliyah Return Center 26 Sep 2026 says "reportedly remained imprisoned".
- No report of release or death found as of 12 Oct 2026. Nothing first-hand since September 2024.
- Safe line: "As far as anyone outside knows, he is still there." / "The last confirmed report, in September 2024, said he was in solitary confinement."
- "Last Jew in Yemen": say "believed to be the last Jew in Yemen" (S1, S10; Ynet says a handful of others may remain, S12).

## Background
- Age of community: Pompeo: Yemeni Jews "have been an important part of Yemen's diverse social fabric for thousands of years" (S6). Safe phrasing: "one of the oldest Jewish communities in the world - there for well over two thousand years, by its own tradition." (No scholarly source read here; keep "by tradition".)
- Operation Magic Carpet ("On Wings of Eagles"): about 49,000 Yemenite Jews flown to Israel, June 1949 - September 1950, from Aden, on British and American transport planes (S11, S12, S4). SAFE: "about 49,000 in 1949-50". Community before: "about 50,000" (S4).
