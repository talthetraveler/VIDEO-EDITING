**The style, in rules Tal has already given (do not relearn them):**

- Wide → **4-frame snap zoom (0.1333s)** → tight hold with a slight continued push → hard cut.
  A zoom he asked for must be VISIBLE (~1.2x → 2.0-2.4x), not a 10% drift.
- **Slow motion `{"speed": 0.5}`** on the handoff / the drink / the bite / the best reaction.
  A slowed beat carries no captions, is `dur/speed` long, and the NEXT beat from that clip must
  start at or after its out-point.
- **One action, one shot.** A second angle of the same action is a repeat. Time only moves forward.
- **The giving must be SEEN**, with Tal in frame where an angle has him (he is a character).
- Reactions: prefer shots where the person is NOT staring into the lens.
- **No title pill, no flash-forward hook, no emoji stickers** on this style unless the item says so.
- **Every in/out on the 30fps frame grid** (multiples of 1/30) — off-grid cuts froze frames ("ITS SO CHOPPY").
- Phone footage that already looks right: `"grade": false`. Never darken a correctly exposed shot.
- **Aim every punch-in on real stills first** (crop the source frame at 4-6 times across the beat)
  — a hand-aimed zoom put a child at the frame edge behind someone's hair.
- Not much talking. Keep only lines that carry the moment. **Never cut anyone off**
  (0.12s before the first word, ~0.45s after the last; a speech RUN ending is not a WORD ending).
- Captions: bold WHITE caps, 1-3 words, held until the sentence ends. Hebrew/Arabic → English
  captions, and **read the original with two models for every span used** (LESSONS 71).
  A translated caption is "timing verified, wording unverified" — say so.
- Nothing negative. Everyone happy. No holiday greetings unless the item wants them.
- "People not passing by" (Tal, item 6): pick takes/crops where no passer-by crosses between
  the camera and the subject or walks through the tight frame.
