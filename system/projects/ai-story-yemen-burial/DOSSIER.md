Paraphrase only (too long to quote): the Facebook post said there were not enough Jews left in Yemen to hold the burial or say
Kaddish, so his neighbours volunteered to give him a dignified farewell (JPost, 16 Jun 2024).

## Safe background phrasing

- Age of the community: "one of the oldest Jewish communities in the world" / "Jews had lived in Yemen since antiquity - some
  two thousand years." Do NOT say "since King Solomon" or "2,500 / 3,000 years" as fact; that is community tradition. (S11)
- Operation Magic Carpet: "In 1949 and 1950 about 49,000 Jews were flown from Yemen to Israel" (some 430 flights; the operation
  ran from late 1948 to September 1950, the mass airlift was June 1949 - September 1950). "Almost 50,000" is safe. (S11)
- By June 2024: "six Jews were known to remain"; "after him, five."

## What to avoid saying
- His age as a number. "Cholera". That Jewish prayers were recited. That he was "the last Jew of Yemen" (he was one of the last).
- Any neighbour's name or words - none were published.
