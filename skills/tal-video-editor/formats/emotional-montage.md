# FORMAT — Emotional Montage

Feeling over information. Music-led. The closest measured relative is the
"call someone you love" phone-booth format, which is the intimate end of this.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title card** | Usually none. If any, a plain white caps card, no pill |
| **Captions** | White, **soft shadow, no stroke**, sentence or caps. **Silent during every real pause** |
| **Hook** | Kind F (peak emotional moment cold-open) or the rig/premise reveal |
| **Pacing** | **Slow. Let moments breathe.** This is the one format where holding is the craft |
| **B-roll** | Only what carries feeling or place |
| **Graphics** | None |
| **Music** | **The spine** — but still under the voice. Stepped envelope, not flat gain |
| **Runtime** | 45–90s |
| **Natural audio** | High. Real pauses are content |

## The rule that matters most in this format

**Captions go silent during real pauses. Never held over dead air.**

Measured independently in two reference videos: Tal's own Damascus Gate sample
had two real pauses (5.4s and 2.4s) with no caption; the third-party reference
had **8 pauses over 1.2s each, all caption-silent.** **[M]**

This is not a stylistic preference. Holding a caption through a silence is the
single most damaging thing you can do to this format — the pause *is* the
emotional beat.

## Always a full story, never a compilation

One subject, start to finish. Do not intercut several people to make it feel
fuller. **[M]**

## Loudness — the calibration point

The successful third-party reference measured **−14.28 LUFS, true peak
−0.03 dBTP** — right at the platform target. Tal's own sample measured −19.7
LUFS even though he held it up as a quality bar, which confirms his posts have
been running 5–7 LU under target. **That is a real fixable gap, not an
overcautious threshold.** Deliver at −14. **[M]**

## Music is optional

No clear music bed was detectable in the successful reference — checked by
spectrogram across two gaps. **The raw voice and the real pauses can carry it.**
Don't add a score reflexively. **[M]**

Where music is used: a stepped volume envelope keyed to the edit's own cut
points — quiet under the cold open, fullest under the emotional anchor, down for
the outro. A flat gain reads as "background music," not scoring. **[M]**

## Close

**Wordless.** No CTA, no final caption competing with the emotional beat. Let
the picture and the sound resolve it. **[M]**
