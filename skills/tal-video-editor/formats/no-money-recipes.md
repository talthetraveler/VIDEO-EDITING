## 2. Voiceover formats he praised

Common to all of them [TAL notes: "Nice voiceover", "copy this type: the voice and everything is a template"]: **one literal shot per sentence**, deadpan, first line is the hook, word-by-word or phrase captions, -14 LUFS with a soft bed, script built from his real lines. Whenever the VO names something, the clip behind it must be exactly that thing [TAL, 2026-10-26]. Also see `no-money-voiceover.md`.

| style | speed | on screen | cut sync |
|---|---|---|---|
| F essay question (DbZWxAVxeP6) | 212-233 wpm, 14-17 word sentences, VO 92-95% of the time, music 6-8 dB under | ONE word at a time, big, centred, lowercase white | only on idea changes; holds 4-14 s; 10-13 shots of him alone and small in ONE place, open and close on the same shot |
| C travel diary, past tense | 206-229 wpm | small serif subtitles lower middle | 1-2.5 s holds; speed-ramp bursts on distance or time lines; a map card |
| D big-brother advice | 164 wpm, 10-11 words a sentence | big kinetic keywords, name in yellow, one-word subs | one literal shot per sentence |
| A soft intro | 184 wpm | word-by-word small captions, centre | sentence ends, 1.8 s average |
| B brisk explainer | 281 wpm | word-by-word white, lower middle | every noun its own shot, about 1.2 s |
| S storytime hook | 198-252 wpm, VO 90-96%, music 3-6 dB under | bold 2-4 word chunks | cut every 1-3 s, say it then see it |
| N YouTube narrator (the quality bars) | 102-146 wpm; VO only 45-64% of the time | serif subtitles, red slam numbers | scenes breathe; VO enters on cuts |

[TAL] which style for which job: life-philosophy reels F (top priority) or D; Day-X hitchhiking legs C; one pinned "who is Tal" intro A or S; long-form N plus live talk to camera; brand work B inside a C or S reel.

Short-form VO runs fast, 200-240 wpm; calm comes from tone and music. Length rule of thumb: 45 s = 140-155 words, 60 s = 190-210.

Praised VO structures (beats and numbers only, no scripts):
- **"So apparently..." second-person life list** (3.3-3.6 words/s, a cut every 1.1-1.5 s). Hook 0-4 s names the decision; beats joined by "but / so / instead / then"; a dry aside every third line; fake ending; the name only at the very end. Captions 2-7 word lowercase chunks, about 2%, 22-25% down. Epic-trailer bed, low.
- **Sarcastic "I just want to say I'm sorry"** (3.4 w/s). Hook 0-2 s with the name; "I did the research"; 2-3 understatements each followed by a reality beat; "it's hard, but..." turn; a 4-5 action list montage; payoff view; one sincere line, one joke, "that was country number N". ONE word at a time, small white bold, about 55% down. Series card at 4-5 s and at the end.
- **One-word cinematic VO** (2.8 w/s, 25-40 s). 4:3 or 5:4 window, shots 1.5-4.8 s, one heavy condensed word dead centre on the subject; twice the frame cuts to a full white card with the word in black (0.3-0.5 s). Problem, turn, feeling, one-line close.
- **"Does it actually work?" local-craft** (85 s, 47 cuts, about 1.8 s). Hook is the question plus "Let's give it a go"; signposted steps (First / Then comes the part that feels wrong / Finally); his own on-camera reaction spliced between steps; a verdict word in bigger italic. Macro hands change every clause.
- **Cinematic essay** (40-100 s, about 150 wpm, 5:4 or 4:3 window, slow 3-6 s shots from behind). Stacked hook lines in mustard-yellow soft serif (about #F2B630) as spoken, or a kinetic word collage; then small lowercase subtitles. Eight beats: hook, setup, common belief, turn, proof, zoom out, ONE joke, bookend.
- **Contrast-hook diary "I'm not ___, but..."** (about 65 s, 9 beats, 3.2 w/s). Cuts land on the picture word; karaoke captions; music 6-8 dB under; loops back to the hook.
- **Flash-forward, then "how did I get here?"** (60-90 s). Payoff moment 0-3 s; the question on a freeze or map zoom-out 3-6 s; rewind with clock words; problems stack; one false ending; back to the opening shot plus one sincere line. Keep the rewind in present tense.
- **Number + stakes, then episodes** (45-90 s). Number and stake in one line, fear said out loud, 3-5 short episodes with a running clock, one honest reflection, a next-episode hook.
- **Problem, helpers, win** (40-90 s). Goal and problem in one breath; each helper gets an intro beat, one ask, one yes, one cut; recap list and a question to the viewer.
- **Cinematic rhythm** of the YouTube-style VO: edit speed mirrors the feeling, fast on lists and joy, long holds on the emotional line.

Words and numbers to say as text, not voice: every price, count and time slams on screen the moment it is said. Never invent one; flag what you cannot verify.

## 3. Cinematic recipes (no voice, music to the last frame)

Universal skeleton across about 120 caption and cinematic reels: **one hook or setup shot 2.5-6.5 s (median 3-4 s) carrying the caption, then a drop into 7-16 clips of 0.4-1.2 s on the beat, 8-18 s total.** The format change often IS the drop (letterbox to full-frame, a caption swap, a black card). Hook holds measured: 2.0 to 11.5 s, most 3-5 s.

| cut grid | where |
|---|---|
| 0.10-0.19 s (3-5 frames) | "So why'd you go" 0.19; flash bursts 0.1-0.3 (median 0.2); risk/regret 0.167 (5 frames); hi-hat bursts 0.12 (24 frames) |
| 0.27-0.5 s | "how was peru" 0.267 (8 frames at 30 fps); "how was japan" 0.42; welcome-variety 0.40; E flicker 0.4-0.5; Welcome-to 0.48 |
| 0.5-0.75 s | why-bring-camera 0.51; Them-going 0.55; why-skateboard 0.63 grid; I-feel-lost 0.6 locked; lyric reels 0.75 |
| 0.8-1.0 s | drone best-of holds 1.1; T10 highlight 0.83; diary 0.88-0.94 |
| 1.0-1.5 s | urge-to-disappear 1.03; South Georgia 1.2 strict; 99.99% 1.1-1.4 |
| 1.9 s | Vietnam film-look quote reel, 12 candid shots at exactly 1.9 s |
| holds | setup 2.3-6.7 s before the drop; closer 3-5 s |

- Hero shot first and last so it loops. Every shot starts already moving: no take-offs, no hovering. People first; a tiny human for scale in wides; low sun.
- Cut ON the beat, never by timer. The first drum hit starts the montage. One breath of silence right before the drop, otherwise wall-to-wall sound. Music plays to the last frame (check the audio stream).
- Tight close-ups of him between the big wides ("the shot with my eyes open"). Quality gate for 0.5 s clips: drop any clip whose 0.6 s window shakes or smears. Shaky footage underperforms, and Tal wants steady.
- **Hold then burst, letterboxed** (T1 "line above the picture"): setup shot 0-2.5 s (max 4) of a person thinking or walking, text a question plus "..."; on the first drop the text swaps to "..." or a one-word payoff; 8-14 peak shots at 0.45-1.0 s on the beat (about 125-130 BPM); hero closer 1.5-2 s; 11-15 s total.
- **Welcome to [COUNTRY]**: small spaced caps "WELCOME TO" over a huge country name, typed on letter by letter or popped on the beat; optionally masked behind a rock or island on a drone push (needs a depth mask). Three timings: FLASH (10-12 shots at 0.13-0.3 s in the first 2 s, 5-6 s drone hold, second burst, then 0.5-1 s), HOLD (6.5 s slow drone push while the title types, then 1.3-1.6 s shots), VARIETY (3.5 s hold under a question, then a cut every 0.40 s). Drone and wide only. Close on the widest shot. 12-19 s.
- **POV: you went to [underrated] instead of [famous]**: drone footage letterboxed (bars about 66%), title typed on over shot 1 for 0.3-2.8 s, shot 1 holds about 2.9 s, then a cut every 0.48 s (about 125 BPM), 14 shots, 10-11 s, loops. Longer form 12-22 s: 1.4-2 s hero opener, a burst of 3-4 shots at 0.4-0.6 s, then 1.2-1.9 s on the beat, a 3-5 s closer. Every shot has a person, low sun or soft overcast.
- **This is [COUNTRY], globe or map reveal**: 4:3 picture in 9:16, no on-screen text; 0-2.6 s one moody hold; 2.0-2.9 s map beat (whip-pan into a spinning globe, or a satellite zoom from space); then 0.4-0.6 s cuts on every beat; 12-13 s. The "99.99% of people will never see this" version: Earth zoom 0-2.9 s with a red pin at about 1.6 s, then 1.1-1.4 s shots.
- **Beat-grid postcard invite** (7-12 s, 11-20 shots of 0.5-0.75 s at constant phase): ONE small line held all reel; every shot a different postcard: wide, static or very slow, level horizon, tiny human in about half; no selfies; same country; golden hour beats midday.
- **Best-drone-shots reel** (13.6 s, 17 shots): hand launch 0-0.77 s, a rounded white pill title, 6 shots x 0.17 s on the drop, then 11 holds of about 1.1 s, one signature move per beat (chase, rising reveal, top-down, orbit, push, silhouette finale), each with a person or vehicle for scale and no two moves alike. Hand-launch clip may only exist in iCloud.
- **Fast-cut styled trailer** (10-15 s, 25-40 shots at 0.35-0.5 s): cuts on the track's audio hits with 1-3 frame flash cuts on the hardest; bookend the same place; one caption line or none; peak-action shots only.
- **"Stories without words"** (15 s): no text; heavy teal-orange grade; 9 flash cuts of 0.15-0.2 s in 0-1.8 s; 12 holds of about 0.78 s, one per beat; a strobe close to black. Order: place from the air, a face, hands, a smile, the site at night, then people doing the culture.
- **Format-switch burst** ("pov: you took traveling the world seriously"): 0-6.0 s one hold in a 16:9 letterbox with a lowercase serif line in the top band; 6.0-8.0 s a burst of 10 shots at 0.15-0.2 s as the picture breaks out to full 9:16; 8.0-19.3 s eleven cinematic shots of exactly 1.0 s.
- **One continuous reveal** (Petra, 12 s): a slow forward push at walking pace, dark narrow approach first, wide and bright at the end; reveal lands by 8-9 s; music frame 1, first hit near 1.5 s, second swell about 6 s later at the reveal. No text. [TAL] "Study this - music, color grading."
- **Passport-and-map tabletop hook** (31 s): VO "one of my favourite countries... let me show you why"; tabletop macro inserts of 0.3-1 s, each landing on a VO word; a black beat; aerial above clouds; then 2-5 s slow holds; score rises after "let me show you why". Tal can shoot the inserts at home in five minutes.
- **Calm to chaos to stillness** (essay reels): edit speed mirrors content. Fast on lists and joy, a 0.3 s burst when the voice describes overthinking, long holds for the confession.

## 7. Explainer about a place, and narrated photo stories

Eight reels measured by Tab on 2026-10-23 (speed 142-238 wpm). The slowest, most personal and relatable hook had by far the most likes (about 9x); the fastest, abstract one the fewest. **These rules are Tab's proposal; Tal has not approved them.** The newer travel rule applies: copy the reference when there is one.
- First word at 0.0 s, no intro, no logo, no music lead-in. Hook sentence 8-15 words, 6-10 spoken in the first 2 s: WHO plus a shocking contrast plus a number. First 1.5 s may flash 3-4 real images at about 0.35 s (Tab would allow this as the one exception to the 1.2 s floor).
- Shape: hook, "Meet X", origin, obstacle, turn, numbers, bookend echoing the hook. No end card; at most one 2 s follow line.
- Narrator 165-185 wpm. A visual change every 0.8-1.5 s (cut, word card, counter or push); nothing static over 2 s. Emphasis card every 6-8 s, 0.4-0.6 s, on the beat word; ONE accent colour per series.
- Photos as framed cards over a blurred copy of themselves with a 3-5% push, never stretched; every money, scale or time number gets a rolling counter; one map zoom at the location beat; one highlighted press card for the most surprising claim.
- Captions 1-3 words, karaoke build, punch word in accent; mid-frame (48-65%) on B-roll, about y 1420 only when a face fills the frame. Bed 8-12 dB under the voice with a whoosh on each card; -14 LUFS, peak at most -1.
- Narrated-photo stories (talking head, photo cutaway every 1-3 s with blur fill, name tag on first mention, hook first, lesson last) use the same grammar.
- Effects menu he told Tab to memorise (kinetic text, text behind the subject, split screen on white, cinematic cutaway, perspective text): use 3-6 at most per reel, usually fewer. VFX stay occasional accents on clean real footage.
