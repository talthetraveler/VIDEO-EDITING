# FORMAT — POV Kindness

Walking up to strangers and treating them well: greetings across religions,
giving something, feeding someone, a small act. **The payoff is always a face
changing.** **[S]**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title card** | Yes. `POV: ...` or `WISHING <group> ...` template, top y ≈ 0.11 |
| **Title hold** | `hook` ~6s, or ~35s if it's one sustained interaction |

| **Pacing** | Quick between greetings (1–2s), **but hold every reaction** |
| **B-roll** | Almost none |
| **Graphics** | Title + captions only |
| **Music** | Low or none. Warmth comes from the people |
| **Runtime** | 30–75s |
| **Natural audio** | **Maximum** |

## Structure

Compilation by nature — many short interactions stronger together than apart.
But **the ordering is a creative act, not a shuffle**: open on the warmest or
most surprising reaction, vary the people, end on the strongest human moment.

**Hook kind D is powerful here:** state the stakes, show "No" ×3, and the
tension becomes *will anyone say yes*. Then the yes lands. **[M]**

## The rule that protects this format

**Greetings score highest in any ranked selection and will hijack the edit.**
That is fine *here* — greetings are the point. It is fatal in `human-story`.
Never let a ranked top-N build a story; here, let it build the montage. **[L]**

## Failure states

- Cutting a reaction short to fit more greetings in
- Music loud enough to flatten the ambience
- Captions running over the silent beat after the handshake — speech-only
- Treating it as a full story. It is a compilation, cut as one
