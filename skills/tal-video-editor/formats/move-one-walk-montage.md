## The idea
One man walks, and every shot is a new country. Each shot holds only 5 frames and the figure is on the SAME spot of the frame (dead centre, small, on the centre line of a symmetrical street, plaza or arcade), so the eye reads one continuous walk across many countries. No VO, no captions, no transitions, no speed ramps, no zooms. Music is the editor. Picture: locked tripod wides, bright daylight, natural slightly warm grade, no grain.

## Rhythm (music)
Original score. Beat about 0.43 s (autocorrelation peak 0.43 s = about 140 BPM in the 6-18 s window; other windows gave harmonics, so read it as "about 140 BPM, cut on the half beat"). Fast sections cut on the EIGHTH note (0.21 s), the middle section on the QUARTER note (0.44 s), the burst on the eighth again, the closing walk-away is one long phrase. The cut rate changes only twice (8th, 4th, 8th) and never wanders. -17.6 LUFS, LRA 15 LU: a dynamic film mix, not a flat social mix.

## What to copy
1. ONE repeatable motif that is the same in every shot (the figure's position and its walk).
2. Three-speed structure: fast wides, half-speed, face burst, one long walk-away, sky, end card.
3. The turn: places first, THEN the face. A smile after 30 places lands harder than opening on one.
4. The title is ONE word and comes back on black at the end.
5. No VO, no captions. The text budget is two words.
