# VOICEOVER + B-ROLL — the shot-by-shot, in words

The companion to `voiceover-broll.md`. That file is the method; this is the
evidence: **every shot of Tal's own film, next to the line spoken over it,
and what is on screen, described in words.** No images are kept in git, so
the pictures are described instead. The contact sheets these descriptions came
from live on the laptop only (`assets/analysis/voiceover-broll/`, rebuild with
`node system/scripts/reference-shots.mjs`).

Job codes (from `voiceover-broll.md` §1): **P** pivot -> his A-roll ·
**N** noun -> literal B-roll · **L** list -> one shot per item ·
**A** abstract -> a receipt · **S** the subject's own voice.

### What the table shows

- **A-roll = 20 of ~85 shots, and every one is on a turn**: "And today", "So
  I started", "And then I stopped hiding", "But honestly", "because we are
  stronger together", "Do you believe that too?", the CTA. Not one A-roll
  shot carries a concrete noun.
- **The list sections cut fastest.** "fed me / drove me / let me sleep": 3
  shots in 4.7s. "where you are from / look like / religion / mother / 
  Palestinian / Israeli / black / white": ~12 shots in 13s.
- **The long holds carry the long sentences**: 5.3s on "no matter their
  religion, no matter their background, no matter their nationality", 3.8s
  of A-roll on "make us forget the human being on the other side".
- **What gets illustrated is the NOUN, not the geography.** "a tribe in the
  Amazon" shows a tribe; "the headlines" shows a Shabbat table. When the
  literal thing doesn't exist in the archive, show the closest true thing
  the words point at, never a mood shot.
- **Receipts for every abstract claim**: placards for "antisemitism" and
  "Islamophobia", real tweets for "hate online", his own IG grid for "a
  billion views", the website for "the initiative".
- **The planted phrase comes back**: "where my mother was born" (0:09) ->
  "it does not matter where your mother is born" (1:45), on the film's most
  joyful shot.

### What Julius adds to the method

- **Two voices, one film.** Tal narrates the facts; Julius says the feelings.
  The feelings get the longest holds in the film (6.5s, 9s).
- **Tal's questions stay in**, every time, because the answer means nothing
  without them.
- **The organisation's name is a chant**, not a caption: shouted with the
  people it serves, when it's introduced and in the last line.
- **Third-party news is used as-is**: letterboxed, chyron and bug intact.
  (Only when Tal supplies it: CLAUDE.md §1 Rights.)
- **Scale by a second face**: one more child ("the 9,000th") turns one story
  into thousands, then the thesis line lands on his face.
