# FORMAT — GIVING THINGS (water, flowers, hugs, notes)

For: `GIVING WATER TO W`, `FLOWERS FOR STRANGERS/*`, `HUG FOR STRANGER/*`,
`NOTES TO STRANGERS`, `SHALOM/SALAM`.

The shape is always: **approach → give → their face.** The gift is the excuse;
the reaction is the video.

## TWO CAMERAS — the thing that makes these work

Tal: *"a long angle shot on an iPhone, and then it switches to the VID which is
shot on the Meta glasses."*

```
WIDE / long lens  ->  lets the viewer SEE what is happening
                      (you walking up, the stranger not yet knowing)
META POV          ->  lets the viewer FEEL it
                      (the hand extending, their face filling the frame)
```

In the roses reference the pattern is literal: a wide of the approach across
the station hall, then a cut to her face as she takes the flowers, then tighter
again as she smiles. **Switch only when the change brings the viewer closer** —
never on a rhythm.

**Zoom into the face for the reaction.** Tal asked for this specifically, for
both water and hospital. A slow push-in on the smile, inside the take.

## CAPTIONS — much lighter here than in a Q&A

The roses reference runs almost **caption-free**. There is nothing to read
because there is nothing being said worth reading — the face is the content.

- Caption only what is actually said and actually matters ("SHANA TOVA",
  "THANK YOU", "GOD BLESS YOU").
- **Never caption an empty reaction shot.** Silence on screen is correct.
- Gold ALL-CAPS, ~72% height, as everywhere else.
- *(2026-09-28: stale. His posted kindness test carries a smaller Hebrew line
  under the English; THE STANDARD allows it when it adds context.)*
- **ENGLISH ONLY.** Tal: *"you shouldn't have captions in Hebrew, only
  English."* Hebrew and Arabic speech gets **translated** (`groqTranslate`),
  never captioned in the original script. The original transcript is kept in the
  cache; only the caption is English.

## CASTING

- **Deliberate visible diversity**, never two similar people in a row.
- **Alternate the greeting** where it applies: Shabbat Shalom → Salam Aleikum.
- **Everyone is happy.** Cut confusion, refusals and awkwardness even when funny.

---

## ROTATION — this footage is MIXED

Several proxies in these shoots are **640x360 landscape containing portrait
content rotated 90° (heads point left)**. Un-rotated they appear sideways.

`scripts/build-edit.mjs` auto-applies `transpose=1` when a landscape proxy
appears in a vertical project. **Always check the contact sheet** — a sideways
shot is instantly visible there and invisible in the code.

---

## VARIATIONS

Tal wants 2-3 variations per shoot. Make them genuinely different, not
reshuffled:
- change **who opens** and **who closes**
- change the **length** (a 25s fast one and a 50s one that breathes)
- change the **emphasis** — one cut built on laughter, one on the quiet
  surprised faces
