# FORMAT — Social Accords / fast talking-head

**This is a FORMAT, not a person.** Tal: *"the montana edit is just an example
for an editing style where it's someone talking to the camera — not only
Montana, just talking to camera."* **[S]**

The fast end of the narrated cut. Same recipe as `nas-explainer`, different cut
rate — one density dial. All numbers measured from Tal's own reference cut
`MONTANA TUCKER V3` (181.5s, 136 shots). **[M]**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title card** | Yellow location / label cards ("LEBANON", "ONLY"), not the white pill |
| **Captions** | **1–2 words per card, snapping on every cut.** White, sentence case, occasional yellow keyword |
| **Hook** | The strongest claim in the VO, stated immediately |
| **Pacing** | **~41 cuts/min** — a cutaway every **1.3–1.5s**. Relentless. 43% of shots under 1s, 79% under 2s |
| **A-roll** | **The speaker is the SPINE, on camera ~50–60%** |
| **B-roll** | Quick INSERTS over and between lines — **never a replacement track** |
| **Graphics** | Heavy: kinetic type, animated counters, evidence-board stacks |
| **Music** | Continuous bed, **−14 LUFS, ~4 LU range** (heavily limited) |
| **Runtime** | 90–200s |
| **Natural audio** | Under the VO |

## The rejected edit — read this first

A ~20 cuts/min version that **removed the speaker and went wall-to-wall B-roll**
was explicitly rejected: *"I was expecting you to make something exactly like
this… do that next time when I tell you."* **[S]**

The mistake was reading "she stays on camera / B-roll everywhere" as *replace
her with B-roll*. **It means the opposite.** She is the continuous spine;
cutaways layer on top. And ~20 cuts/min "felt dead" against the reference's 41.

## The eight devices

1. **Machine-gun cutaways** over a continuous A-roll spine
2. **Evidence-board photo stacks** on a paper ground — `src/components/evidence/`
   (`PaperBackdrop`, `PhotoPrint`, `PhotoStack`, `RedLabelBlock`, `DateStamp`,
   `HandScribble`, `HandPie`). Warm paper, hand-drawn marker circles and arrows
3. **Kinetic typography** — big bold single words ("BREAK", "TREND")
4. **Animated number counters** — "14,000,000", "3 BILLION"
5. **Tracked phone screen** as a recurring motif (feed cards changing in-hand)
6. **Yellow location/label title cards**
7. **News / conflict footage** as thematic B-roll, arranged as photo cards
8. **Photo stills** for family beats, with hand-drawn circles

## Practical notes

- **Burned-in captions on source reels don't matter at 1.5s/shot.** The
  reference uses the exact clips that were once spent hours being cropped clean.
  Stop fighting them; cut fast. **[S]**
- External footage is fair game here if Tal's brief says so — confirm the source
  before pulling. **[S]**
- The real "SOCIAL ACCORDS" monogram (dark + gold) must come from Tal. **[S]**
