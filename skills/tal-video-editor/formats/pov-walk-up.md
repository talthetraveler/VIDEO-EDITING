# FORMAT — POV walk-up interview (the Nissim formula)

From Tab's Editing Handbook v2 (2026-11-01). Taken from "POV: Meeting a Black Jew in Israel", about
1.5M views. **Measured by Tab from the edit, not re-measured here.** The reasons it worked are Tab's
guesses; the numbers are read from the cut. Tab has not compared the approved file with the post.

Street POV only. Nothing here applies to a travel video (`no-money-travel.md`).

## The formula

1. **Open on the walk-up, never a teaser.** First frame: the person from behind or side-on, from his
   POV, with his hand or voice coming in: "Hey, excuse me." No later clip in front. Tal rejected
   that on this video: *"it doesn't really make sense."*
2. **A person with a visible contradiction,** named in the title in six words or fewer: "POV: Meeting
   a ___". White rounded box, black bold italic condensed, top of frame, 0 to about 3.6 s.
3. **First question by 1.5 s:** a yes/no identity question.
4. **Say the contradiction out loud by about 0:08:** his "Wait, so you're a ___?" and their
   confirmation. Push in on the answer and keep their shortest quotable line.
5. **First 12-15 s are one continuous take.** No music, no sound effects, no coloured words.
6. **Second twist by 0:15-0:25,** their backstory, right where people swipe away. He repeats it back
   in one sentence.
7. **Back half:** "Tell me about yourself" → "What makes you happy?" → "If you could send one message
   to the world?". He is on camera once. Cut only when the question or speaker changes.
8. **End warm:** a hug or handshake, a warm goodbye, then the end card. No call to action. 60-80 s.

## The cut, measured

| what | number |
|---|---|
| length | 1:17, talk to 1:13, then a 4 s end card |
| cuts | 0 in the first 15 s, then about 13 in 58 s, one every 4.5 s |
| push-ins | five, slow, 1.15-1.18×, on the biggest lines |
| shortest shot | 1.2 s |
| first words | 0.0 s; first question 1.2 s; the turn 2.2 s |
| audio | raw phone sound, lifted to reel loudness; no music, no effects |
| colour | natural |

**Captions:** one layer, sentence case, pure white, extra-bold sans, soft drop shadow, lower third, at
most 2 lines, hard cut in and out, at least 0.8 s each. No coloured words, no pop-ups, no emojis. Both
speakers the same. This overrides THE STANDARD's caps for this format, because this is what the
posted cut did.

**Plainest won.** Five versions were made. The one with yellow key words, title cards, pop-ups and
whooshes was stripped back for the final to match the approved reference.

## Hook template

```
TITLE (0-3.5 s): POV: Meeting a [identity that sounds surprising]
0.0 s  [person from behind or side-on]  "Hey, excuse me. Sorry."
1.2 s  "Are you [A]?"  ->  "Yeah, I'm [A]."
3 s    "Wow, nice to meet you, man. What's your name?"  ->  [name]
5.5 s  "Wait, so you're a [A + B]?"  ->  "I'm a [A + B]."  [push in]  + their short line
11 s   "Wait, tell me your story."  ->  [the second twist, by 0:25]
```

change is re-cutting them to open on the walk-up. The per-video list of titles and first lines is in
