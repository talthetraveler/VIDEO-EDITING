# COMPILATION ORDER — who appears, in what order. A hard rule.

Tal, 2026-09-21: *"Don't start with the same girl for the video you're gonna
edit. Start with someone else, like a religious Jew. Then go to a Muslim. Then
this and that. Like you wanna show the diversity, of the black guy."*

This decides the running order of every compilation — greetings, Q&A, giving
things, kindness tests. It is not a garnish on the edit; it **is** the edit.
The point of the channel is *"showing you Israel beyond headlines"*, and the
proof is who is on screen, one after another.

---

## 1. THE RULES

1. **Never open on whoever opened the last version.** A new variation that
   starts with the same face is a reshuffle, not a variation. If V1 opened on
   the young woman, V2 opens on the religious Jewish man.
2. **Never two similar people back to back.** Not two religious Jewish men, not
   two young women, not two older men — no matter how good both answers are.
   Split them with someone visibly different and the good one still lands.
3. **Alternate deliberately and visibly.** The sequence people feel is:
   `religious Jew → Muslim woman → Black man → secular young woman →
    older Arab man → soldier → child → tourist`
   Not that exact list every time — that *kind* of movement, every time.
4. **Diversity is visual, not a caption.** Nobody is ever labelled on screen.
   No "Muslim" / "Jewish" tag. You see it; you are never told it.
5. **The strongest answer does NOT open the video.** It goes third or fourth,
   once the viewer is already in. Opening on the best line spends it before
   anyone is watching — and the strongest is usually the longest, which is the
   worst possible hook.
6. **Everyone is happy.** A flat, bored or reluctant answer comes out, however
   diverse the person is. Rule 7 of the bible beats this whole file.

## 2. HOW TO ORDER A COMPILATION

```
1  SHORTEST, WARMEST, most surprising face  (the hook — under 2s to a smile)
2  someone VISIBLY different from #1
3  the strongest answer in the folder
4  a laugh, or a child
5  someone visibly different again
…  alternate, never repeating a type within two slots
N  end on the warmest human moment, not the last thing filmed
```

## 3. MAKING REAL VARIATIONS

Tal: *"You can make a bunch of different short variations. You have full
creative control."*

A variation changes **who starts, who appears, the order, the hook, the length
and the ending** (CLAUDE.md §2.3). Concretely, for one folder:

- **V1** — the widest spread of people, opening on a religious Jewish man.
- **V2** — opens on a completely different person, shorter, faster cuts, only
  the funniest/warmest answers.

Two genuinely different cuts beat five reshuffles. If a second variation would
only move clips around, ship one and say so.
