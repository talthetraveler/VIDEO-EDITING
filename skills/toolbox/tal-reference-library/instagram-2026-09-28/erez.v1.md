## Style A — kindness set-piece to a song (5 reels, 108K–237K, median 166K)

A recurring performer (identity not verified as Erez) sets up a surprise for a stranger, usually a soldier.
1. **Frame 0.0 s:** performer, target and prop (flowers / basket / tray) all in frame; a premise card at once, often "פוב:" ("POV:" used as the meme, not first-person footage).
2. **Setup 0–15 s:** prop laid out; the target stays deadpan, labelled with an **emoji sticker placed on the target's body** (😳 🤭 😋).
3. **~20–27 s:** comment-bait card — "תרימו בתגובות ל…" ("cheer them in the comments") / "חכו לסוף" ("wait for the end").
4. **The turn:** smile, reading the note, eating, a toast → sticker switches to 🥹 / 🤩.
5. **Last 5–8 s:** hug / toast / shared meal → thesis card ("ישראל ביחד חזקים", ≈"Israel together strong") → "שתפו ותעקבו" ("share and follow").

Measured: 32–60 s. **21–35 cuts/min on one handheld camera; 62–73/min with a locked wide + a tight handheld** at 0.3–1.0 s shots (two-camera = estimate). A Hebrew song with lyrics throughout, no audible dialogue. Text: 3–10-word cards, 1–3 lines, held 1–10 s, white with soft shadow, apparently Instagram's native rounded sans (estimate), line height ≈4.5–5 % of H, centre y≈0.60–0.75.

## Transferable techniques for Tal

1. **Emoji sticker on the subject's body at the reaction beat** — 😳 while waiting, 🥹/🤩 on the smile. Labels emotion where Tal's rules leave reaction beats uncaptioned, without covering the face. Needs a sticker layer; not known whether the pipeline has one.
2. **The prop/intent in the first frame**, stranger already there (flowers or note in hand, not the walk-up).
3. **Open on the spoken question**, first words inside 1.2 s — agrees with the cold-open rule in `street-interview.md`.
4. **A specific comment-prompt card** early (0–3 s) or mid (20–27 s): "tell me where you're from", not "like and follow".
5. **Make the stranger the hero** — their generosity is the payoff, not Tal's gift.
6. **Thesis card last, then the CTA** — fallback when nobody says the thesis on camera.
7. **One slow-motion hold (~1.5 s)** on the best reaction.
8. **−14 LUFS confirmed** — matches Tal's pipeline.

## Do not transfer

Songs replacing dialogue (Tal's formats rate natural audio high; any song needs a licence) · sentence-length cards in place of speech captions · pixelated faces · 60+/min two-camera cutting (Tal's measured 15–30/min already matches Erez's single-camera 21–35) · military/patriotic framing.
