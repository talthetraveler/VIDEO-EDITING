## 4. Limits

- n small, 1–2 videos per style; views read once at post ages 0–12 days; no retention data; collabs may have reached a second audience.
- Cut counts ±10–15 %. The detector over-counted handheld and screen recordings 2–6×, under-counted letterboxed footage (Ddi7 14 vs ~19; Ddju3 estimator said 2, frames show 7).
- Transcripts: Groq missed ~18s in Ddr6, hallucinated "Thank you" over music in Ddju3, returned song lyrics in DdeL.
- Music detection: stereo side level didn't separate music from phone-stereo ambience; gap spectrograms used instead, checked against the known song and the music-only clip. Reliable for "yes"; too-short gaps → "undetermined", not "no".
- Caption heights ±2–3 % (off 216-px sheet cells); font names are guesses by eye.
- Resolution is Instagram's served variant, which may differ from what he uploaded.
