## What all 9 share

- A face on frame 1, already talking; first word by 0.14s.
- Claim + scale hook within 3–8s.
- The picture shows the noun being said: one shot per list item at 0.5–1.2s, including stop-motion product swaps (19 frames in 2s in Ddly).
- The host appears inside the B-roll (ironing, lying on the massage table, sitting in the wheelchair).
- Guests speak script lines to camera (6/9).
- AI imagery for what can't be filmed (4/9).
- White/light-leak flash at section changes (8/9); some carry a stereo whoosh, SFX otherwise sparse.
- A misdirection/question beat before the reveal ("It's not for a Prime Minister… it's for a document").
- End on a callback or value line, or a one-shot-per-item recap montage, then a ~3s end card.
- Sound identical across all 9: −14.0 to −14.2 LUFS, continuous music bed ~14 dB under (median), no silence ≥0.3s before the end card.
- Nas is not always the host: one female presenter, one co-host (@aija), two with partners/sponsors as co-hosts.

## Styles

**A — access story (NASDAILY. end card)** — 5 videos, median 1.9M, all three top videos. 31 cuts/min, 141 wpm.
1. 0–3s on location, host + subject or crowd; show the payoff physically.
2. 3–8s hook line; optional yellow punch word; quick reaction burst.
3. Flash → the subject by name and credential.
4. The problem shown literally (AI where unfilmable).
5. A pivot / misdirection question.
6. Flash → process/proof, one step per shot, host taking part, guest soundbites.
7. The scale number.
8. Flash → values line or callback over a montage of different people together.
9. NASDAILY. end card.

**B1 — desk list / AI tutorial (NAS.COM WEEK N)** — 1.4M, 536K; 22.5–31 cuts/min, ~131 wpm.
1. Nas at a desk, number/claim hook (green counter or punch word).
2. Per item: a full-screen numbered yellow title card for 2.5–7s → the demo as a screen recording (sometimes an inset card on a starfield), typed prompts held 8–11s → his reaction with floating graphics.
3. Recap montage → a question to the viewer → "See you next week".

**B2 — offer pitch (NAS.COM)** — 751K, 551K; 24–27 cuts/min.
1. Qualifier or offer hook.
2. Colour-coded features over Nas on a stool, UI/mascot overlays, client montage.
3. Partner credibility + scarcity.
4. "Comment X" + URL.

**The top two show the ending in the first seconds and pay that image off at
the end.** The #2 (6.1M) is sponsored but built as a story, not a pitch.
(9 videos of different ages: a pattern, not proof.)
