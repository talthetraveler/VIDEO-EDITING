## 2. The constant template

- **Open:** no title card or text hook; the first spoken line captioned from frame 0. **7/10 open on a ~3–4× punched-in face that pulls out to the wide in ~0.25 s** (the 3 without it are the 3 oldest).
- **First line = a question or an ask:** "Who made this?" · "Would you rather have a brand new Spider-Man or $1,000 cash?" · "You can't hang out here…" · "Do you want this umbrella?"
- **Runtime at the 3-minute cap** (9/10 run 163–180 s, often ending mid-sentence; the 42.1M is 105 s). No outro; the only CTA is a small `*Crowdfund Link In Bio*` tag (3/10).
- **Repeated questions:** name / dream (6/10) / single mom? / message to the world / can I give you a hug?
- **Escalation ladder ("one more surprise"), 2–4 reveals per video:** cash → sponsor or celebrity → $10k / a car / a year's rent. **6/10 end with the recipient passing the kindness on** — the same ending as Tal's own cuts.
- **Talk is jump-cut hard** (speech fills 83–94 % of runtime; median shot 1.5–4.5 s); **the reveal is one unbroken shot of 7–18 s.**
- **Captions:** sentence case, bold condensed sans (by eye), pure yellow (#FAFA00) or white, ~3 px black stroke, no box; centred **y ≈ 0.575**; max 2 lines, ~26 chars/line, **always ending in "..."**; 34–57 captions/min, 3.4–4.6 words each, ~1.1–1.8 s on screen. **Yellow marks the hook line (7/10) and the payoff lines — not the speaker.** A small yellow asterisk line underneath: `*10 minutes later*`, `*Calls her mom*`, `*Angie, co-founder of Angi*`.
- **Music:** a stereo bed that **swells 3–9 dB at the main reveal** (7/10), dips (2) or is **cut out entirely for the reveals** (1). **In 3/10 the open is dry and the bed enters at 16–19 s, exactly at the story turn.**

## 3. Styles

**A. Hidden-camera test** (Dc3oi, Dc1bi, Dcgs, Dcyh, DcTZ, DcZS — 2.7–42.1M)
1. 0–3 s hook: the first line.
2. 0–12 s the test: a need (barefoot in the rain, "stepped on glass") or a choice ("$1k for you or give $10k away?") — long lens from a parked car (sometimes visible).
3. 5–30 s the why, in their words; time jumps covered by `*10 minutes later*`, a pan across the blurred car, or a white flash.
4. Identity: name, dream, hardship; 2–4 s jump cuts.
5. First reveal at 26–67 % of runtime, **held 7–12 s**, captions turn yellow.
6. 1–3 escalations ("one more surprise").
7. End on a hug / the stranger's gratitude line, cut at the cap.

**B. Celebrity / blindfold escalation** (DdbdeE, Dcbhx, Dcwty — 8.5–44.6M)
1. Handheld crew, open mid-action on a line.
2. A small first kindness, reaction held 8.5–18 s.
3. A celebrity is brought in.
4. Blindfold walk while the recipient retells their story → "One, two, three… turn around."
5. Car / $10k reveal held 9–10 s.
6. Crowd montage at 0.5–1 s per shot with the subject's voice over it.

**C. Chain game** (DceTT): the same game (flip a coin for $500) with 3 strangers in a row, music removed for each reveal.

**What the top 3 do differently (hypothesis, n=10):** the first reveal is held
longer — **9.8–18 s, mean 13.3 vs 7.8** for the other 7 — lands at 26–63 % of
runtime, with a stake in the first sentence. Cut rate, wpm, caption colour,
sponsorship and runtime don't separate them. Views confounded by post age,
celebrity reach and possible paid promotion (the 23.1M rank is the least trustworthy).

## 4. Against the presets

**Transfers:** the stranger's why right after the decision, their last words end
the video (= Tal's V4 coffee cut) · jump-cut the talk, hold the reveal (Tal's
11–15 s holds already sit inside MD's top-3 range) · 12–25 cuts/min confirmed
(median ≈19) · the kindness passed on · a dry open, bed entering at the story
turn, swell (or pull the music) at the reveal (= Tal's V4 "rising stereo
layer") · long lens for the ask, close camera for the reveal · time-jump tags /
wipes · the 0.25 s punch-out opener (not in the pipeline; test against these
frames first) · yellow only for hook and payoff lines (matches CLAUDE.md rule 3).

**Contradicts:** runtime "under 60 s" vs MD's 105–180 s — MD's length comes from
2–4 reveals, so longer is only supported when the footage has a real second act ·
sentence-case phrases vs THE STANDARD — keep the standard; keep the
colour-for-hook/payoff logic, not the font · giving-things' "one person = 3–6
cuts/min" came from a near-silent reel; MD's talking one-person stories run
12–28/min · "push in as they speak" was not seen as a consistent MD device (not measured).

**Don't transfer:** fake disability or injury as the test (DcTZ, Dcyh) · the
money / sponsor / celebrity escalation · true peaks over 0 dBFS.

## 5. How each measurement was checked

- The tool's detector over-counts; a first custom detector counted a one-frame brightness dip every ~20 frames in Dcgs (luma 73.7 vs 99.7) as cuts, so it was replaced with one that normalises each frame and rejects one-frame flashes. Eye checks: Dc3oi 22–23 vs 22 counted; DdbdeE dn ≥ 0.5 candidates all real; Dcgs rain/flicker false positives below threshold; Dcbhx ~5/45 whip/zoom false positives (hence ranges). It can miss cuts into fast motion (one confirmed by eye at DdbdeE 79.1 s).
- Caption counts ±15 % (16 detected vs 14 by eye, DdbdeE 0–12 s). Colour checked against the Dc3oi sheets.
- Music inferred from stereo side energy: hard evidence in the 3 videos with digital-silent stretches (which end exactly at story turns); "bed throughout" for the other 7 is an estimate.
- wpm from Groq with loops removed. Views = rounded grid counts. Font by eye. The wireless mic on Zach is inferred.
- **−14 LUFS here is Instagram's normalisation of what it serves** (see montanatucker/FINDINGS.md), not proof of his mix.
- Not done: listening, playback, zoom measurement, automatic caption-text reading.
